ABOUT VALUERADAR

Shopping research without the noise.

ValueRadar India is an independent product-discovery and shopping research project for Indian shoppers.

We use AI-assisted research to help organise product information, specifications, marketplace feedback, usefulness, alternatives and price/value signals. The goal is to make it easier to decide what is worth checking — and what may be worth skipping.

How we approach recommendations

We do not treat every discount badge as a deal. Our research can result in labels such as Good Value, Genuine Deal, Useful Find or Skip. A recommendation is not a guarantee that a product is suitable for every shopper.

Independence

Commercial relationships or affiliate links do not determine a ValueRadar verdict. If affiliate links are used, they will be disclosed. Prices, ratings, reviews, availability and marketplace information can change, so shoppers should verify the final listing before purchasing.
