# Affiliate data integration plan

After approval and API/feed eligibility:
- ingest only data permitted by the applicable affiliate license;
- preserve marketplace identifiers such as ASIN/PID when available;
- store timestamps for volatile price/availability facts;
- keep affiliate URL generation separate from editorial verdict logic;
- never let commission rate automatically determine a positive verdict;
- use revenue data to prioritize research, not to falsify product quality/value claims.
