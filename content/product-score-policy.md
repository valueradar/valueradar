# ValueRadar scoring policy

Scores are editorial research signals, not laboratory measurements.

## Dimensions
- Usefulness: does the product solve a clear everyday problem?
- Customer feedback: strength and scale of available marketplace feedback, with caution for recency/quality.
- Value: usefulness/features relative to the observed or tracked price context.
- Features: practical feature set for the intended use case.
- Overall: editorial synthesis of the above, not a simple promise of product quality.

## Verdict gates
- GOOD_VALUE: convincing usefulness/value without evidence of an exceptional historical deal.
- USEFUL_FIND: solves a clear problem but value evidence is less decisive.
- HIGHLY_RATED: unusually strong marketplace feedback signal; not a guarantee of quality.
- PRICE_DROP: requires defensible historical-price evidence.
- GENUINE_DEAL: requires stronger independent price-history/value evidence than a marketplace discount badge.
- SKIP: current evidence does not justify recommending the product.
