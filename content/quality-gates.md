# Publishing quality gates

A product may be marked `published` only when:
- identity/model is sufficiently clear;
- direct or clearly relevant marketplace destination is available;
- shopper use case is clear;
- limitations/watch-outs are included;
- research date is recorded;
- price source/context is explicit;
- rating evidence is not presented as a guarantee;
- verdict is supported by the available evidence;
- SEO title/description are original and useful;
- no unsupported urgency, discount or authenticity claim is made.

If evidence is incomplete, keep the record as `draft` or reject the candidate.
