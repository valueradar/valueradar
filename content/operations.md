# ValueRadar operating model

Keep manual work focused on actions that require account ownership or judgment.

Automate/reuse:
- product schema validation;
- static page generation;
- sitemap generation;
- campaign URL construction;
- content queue formatting;
- analytics event instrumentation.

Keep human/account-owner controlled:
- affiliate enrollment and legal acceptance;
- payment/tax identity steps;
- third-party media rights confirmation;
- external paid purchases;
- social account authorization when required.
