# Catalogue milestones

- 4 published products: first usable catalogue and traffic-test set.
- 10 published products: affiliate-readiness review and first structured buying guide.
- 25 published products: category-level conversion comparison.
- 50 published products: prune/update low-value pages and scale winning categories.
- 100 quality pages: SEO/content-commerce portfolio stage, not a target for thin pages.

Quality and conversion evidence outrank page count at every milestone.
