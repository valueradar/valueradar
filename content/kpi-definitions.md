# KPI definitions

- Qualified visit: a ValueRadar visit reaching relevant product/category content.
- Marketplace CTR: marketplace clicks / product-page views.
- Affiliate conversion: attributed orders / affiliate clicks, using affiliate-program reporting.
- Commission per click: attributed commission / affiliate clicks.
- Commission per 1,000 ValueRadar visits: attributed commission / ValueRadar visits × 1,000.

Use these to choose future products/categories. Social reach is an input metric, not the final success metric.
