# Social publishing checklist

Before publishing any ValueRadar product post:

- Verify the destination ValueRadar page is live.
- Re-check marketplace price/availability if a price is mentioned in the creative.
- Use the VR code visibly.
- Prefer `valueradar.in/products/<slug>/` as the CTA destination.
- Add campaign UTMs when a social-posting workflow is connected.
- Do not claim scarcity, lowest price or exceptional discount without evidence.
- Do not reuse marketplace images/video unless permitted by the applicable rights/license.
- Keep a clear distinction between ValueRadar's verdict and marketplace marketing labels.
