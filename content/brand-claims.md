# Brand claims

Preferred:
- AI-assisted research
- Useful finds and value signals
- Marketplace price observed on [date]
- Tracked reference price
- Highly rated marketplace signal
- Verify current marketplace details before buying

Avoid without strong evidence:
- Best product
- Lowest price
- Best price ever
- 100% trusted/original
- Guaranteed deal
- Genuine deal based only on displayed MRP discount
- Fake scarcity or urgency
