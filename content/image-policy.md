# Product image policy

Product imagery must be usable with confidence.

Preferred order:
1. ValueRadar/user-created original product photo or video frame.
2. Image explicitly licensed/permitted for affiliate or promotional use under the applicable program terms.
3. Brand/manufacturer media only when usage rights permit it.
4. Placeholder until compliant media is available.

Do not generate an AI approximation of a specific commercial product and present it as an authentic product photo. Do not copy marketplace imagery merely because it is publicly visible.
