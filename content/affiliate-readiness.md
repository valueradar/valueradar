# Affiliate readiness checklist

Do not rush enrollment merely to obtain links. Build a credible content footprint first.

## Before enrollment
- [x] Custom domain and HTTPS
- [x] About, privacy, disclosure, disclaimer and terms pages
- [x] GA4 and Search Console
- [x] Product research methodology
- [x] First four product pages prepared
- [ ] Reach roughly 10 strong original product/review pages
- [ ] Publish supporting social content consistently
- [ ] Confirm marketplace links and old research snapshots before promotion

## After approval
- Add the required affiliate disclosure language.
- Replace normal marketplace links only with compliant affiliate links generated by the approved program/tools.
- Set `affiliate: true` only for actual affiliate URLs so generated pages use `rel="sponsored"`.
- Re-check analytics events after link migration.
- Never use personal/family purchases to manufacture qualifying sales.
