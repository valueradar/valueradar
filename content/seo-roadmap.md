# SEO roadmap

After the first 10 product pages, expand around shopper intent rather than publishing thin product copies.

Priority formats:
- Best useful products under ₹500 / ₹1,000
- Product A vs Product B comparisons
- Category buying guides with original ValueRadar reasoning
- Price/value explainers
- Practical use-case lists
- Update pages when meaningful price/specification changes occur

Every guide should link with normal crawlable `<a href>` links to relevant ValueRadar product pages. Avoid thin affiliate pages that merely repeat marketplace descriptions.
