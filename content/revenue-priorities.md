# Revenue prioritization

Candidate score should consider:
1. Purchase intent
2. Clear usefulness/problem solved
3. Commission-category economics
4. Social demonstration potential
5. Search/content potential
6. Marketplace feedback quality/volume
7. Price accessibility
8. Evidence quality and ease of refreshing

Avoid overweighting ticket price. A cheaper product with stronger intent, conversion and commission rate can outperform a high-priced low-commission product.
