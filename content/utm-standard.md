# ValueRadar UTM standard

Use lowercase values consistently.

- `utm_source`: instagram | facebook | youtube
- `utm_medium`: social
- `utm_campaign`: vr###-product-short-name
- `utm_content`: reel | short | post | story

Example pattern:
`https://valueradar.in/products/<slug>/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=vr002-agaro-chopper&utm_content=reel`

This allows GA4 to connect social traffic to individual ValueRadar products before affiliate conversion data becomes available.
